# Paper Presentations and Discussion

Each student is assigned one paper that they are responsible for leading the
presentation and discussion of. This is 20% of your course grade.

- [Guidance and Structure](guidance.md): what to prepare and how the session
  runs
- [Rubric](rubric.md): how the session is graded

## The essentials

| | |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 45 to 60 minutes |
| **Slides due** | Noon on the Thursday before your session, by email |
| **Late penalty** | 5 points per day, up to 20 points |

## If you are not presenting

You are still expected to have read the paper and to come with something to
say. A discussion leader can only work with what the room brings.
