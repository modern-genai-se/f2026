# Paper Presentations and Discussion

- Each student will lead one paper presentation and discussion session. Each paper session should last roughly 50 minutes, and has three parts: a 15-minute presentation, a 10-minute Reviewer's Card activity, and a 25-minute discussion.

- **NOTE: You are responsible for sending the instructor your completed slide deck via email by noon on the Thursday before your session. Five points will be deducted from your presentation grade for each day the slides are late, up to a maximum of 20 points.**
- Name the subject line of your email "GenAI Seminar Slides – [first author] – [your presentation date]". For example: GenAI Seminar Slides – Butler – Sep 14.
