# Schedule

Readings are assigned one week ahead. Printed copies are handed out at the end
of the preceding class, and PDFs are posted on Blackboard. This page is the
authoritative version and is updated as the semester goes.

Development, **E** = Evaluating Tools and Claims, **L** = GenAI Tools Across
the Development Lifecycle.
