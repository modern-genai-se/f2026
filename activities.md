# Hands-On Activities

Four hands-on activities are completed during class over the course of the semester. Together they are worth 20% of the course grade.

Activity dates are announced in advance on the [Schedule](schedule.md). **Bring a laptop on those days.**

Each activity ends with a short handwritten reflection that is collected before you leave and. Reflections are graded out of 10 points. Because the activity happens in class, late reflections are not accepted.
